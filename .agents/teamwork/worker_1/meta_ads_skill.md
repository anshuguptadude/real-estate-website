## Mode A: Campaign & Ad Set Structure (The 2026 Blueprint)

### 1. The Core Rule: Prevent Budget Fragmentation
For daily budgets under ₹1,500/day (e.g., ₹300–₹600/day):
* ❌ **NEVER** create multiple campaigns or multiple ad sets. Splitting ₹500 across 3 campaigns gives ₹160/campaign, which completely starves Meta's machine learning algorithm.
* ❌ **NEVER** launch separate campaigns for each new ad variation or creative test.
* ✅ **MANDATORY STRUCTURE**:
  - **1 Campaign**: Single consolidated objective (Leads, Sales, or Engagement).
  - **1 Ad Set**: 100% of the daily budget pooled into one learning bucket.
  - **3 to 4 Diversified Creatives**: Attached directly inside that single Ad Set.

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                  │          1 CAMPAIGN           │
```

### 2. Targeting Setup in 2026
1. **The Creative IS the Targeting**: Meta's AI scans video audio, on-screen text, transcriptions, and ad copy to locate the highest-intent buyers automatically.
2. **Advantage+ Audience**: Keep targeting broad. Avoid stacking 15 restrictive interest tags.
3. **Location**: Set precise geographic radius if delivery or service is local (e.g., Agra + 25km radius).
4. **Value Rules**: Utilize Meta's Value Rules to deprioritize or exclude low-converting age brackets or demographics that never convert.
5. **Placements**: Select **Advantage+ Placements** by default to let Meta allocate spend across Feeds, Stories, and Reels based on lowest CPM and highest conversion efficiency.

## Mode B: Creative Engine & Ad Copywriting

Every campaign must launch with **3 to 4 distinctly diversified angles** to allow Meta's algorithm to test and find the winner:

1. **Angle 1 — UGC / Real-Life Demo (9:16 Vertical Reel)**:
   - Authentic, unpolished mobile video showing unboxing or direct drop-in installation.
   - Shows the immediate sensory experience and 100% DIY proof.
2. **Angle 2 — Social Proof / Customer Testimonial**:
   - Real customer quote, before-and-after experience, or screenshot proof solving a tangible pain point.
3. **Angle 3 — Pain-Point / Problem-Solving Hook**:
   - Focuses squarely on the annoying cost/damage of the problem (e.g., hard water hair fall, white stains on taps, burnt geyser coils).
4. **Angle 4 — Clean Benefit Graphic / Carousel**:
   - High-contrast visual highlighting the top 3 USPs, pricing/free delivery offer, and clear WhatsApp CTA.

### The 4-Part Video Script Blueprint
```
[00:00 - 00:03] THE HOOK: Visual pattern interrupt + audience calling card.
[00:03 - 00:10] THE PROBLEM (AGITATION): Show why traditional alternatives fail or cause headaches.
[00:10 - 00:20] THE SOLUTION: Introduce product as the effortless 1-step fix.
[00:20 - 00:30] THE OFFER & CTA: Urgency, doorstep delivery, direct WhatsApp button.
```

## Mode C: Metrics Diagnostic Tree (Post-Launch Audit)

### The 72-Hour Golden Rule
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DO NOT touch or edit ads during the first 3 to 4 days (72–96 hours).**
> Any budget change, ad pause, or text edit during this window resets Meta's machine learning algorithm back to the initial Learning Phase.

### Diagnostic Evaluation Matrix:
| Metric | Healthy Benchmark | If Below Benchmark | Fix Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook Rate** (3s Views / Impressions) | > 25% – 30% | Hook is boring or slow | Change first 3 seconds, add bold caption overlay, use pattern interrupt. |
| **CTR (Link Click-Through Rate)** | > 1.5% – 2.0% | Ad copy/creative lacks desire | Sharpen offer, improve CTA clarity, highlight core USP earlier. |
| **CPC (Cost Per Link Click)** | Low (under ₹5–₹15 depending on niche) | High CPC indicates low ad relevance | Test a simpler, more direct creative angle or broaden audience. |
| **Hold Rate** (ThruPlays / 3s Views) | > 20% – 25% | Viewers drop off in the middle | Trim video pacing, remove fluff, get straight to the demo. |
| **Conversion Rate** (Leads / Clicks) | > 5% – 10% | Landing page or WhatsApp flow drop-off | Streamline WhatsApp greeting message or optimize destination page speed. |

## Mode D: Scaling & Fatigue Management

1. **How to Scale Winning Ads**:
   - When a creative consistently delivers low CPA / positive ROI over 4+ days, scale budget by **15% to 20% every 48–72 hours**.
   - ❌ Never double or triple budget overnight (e.g. jumping from ₹500 to ₹1,500 causes CPA to spike and resets learning).
2. **How to Handle Underperformers**:
   - If Meta spends ₹0 or minimal budget on a creative after 4 days, or if CTR is < 0.8% with zero conversions, **pause that single ad**.
3. **Combating Creative Fatigue**:
   - When frequency rises above 2.5–3.0 and CTR starts dropping, do NOT restart the campaign.
   - Simply add **1–2 fresh creative variations** directly into the existing Ad Set. Meta will seamlessly shift budget to the fresh creative without resetting campaign maturity.
