## Retrospective Notes
- **What worked**: The dual-iteration Project Pattern with independent Challengers and Forensic Auditors proved invaluable. Challenger 2 caught substantive legal and statutory vulnerabilities (such as the repeal of Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 by the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, Order 37 summary suit inapplicability, family straw-buyer privity loopholes, and Bhulekh scraping risks) that generic reviews missed. Hardening the document in Iteration 2 elevated `docs/marketing_strategy.md` to an authoritative, enterprise-grade strategic standard.
- **What didn't**: Initial reliance on standard civil court litigation mechanisms (Order 37 CPC) under-estimated backlogs in the Agra District Court (5-10 years), which was successfully replaced with fast-track Sole Arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
- **Lessons learned**: Real estate marketing blueprints in Tier-2 Indian cities require tight integration between marketing tech (Meta CAPI, WhatsApp Webhooks) and local statutory frameworks (UP Revenue Code Sec 80, ADA Master Plan 2031, UP RERA 2016, and BSA 2023).
